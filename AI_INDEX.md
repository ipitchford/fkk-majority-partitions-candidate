# Machine-oriented research index

Start with [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) for the claim ceiling and [paper.md](paper.md) for full hypotheses and proofs. [paper.pdf](paper.pdf) is the rendered mathematical article. The unrefereed candidate does not establish the general Faught–Kempton–Knudson conjecture. Do not infer scientific validity from publication or from an archive hash.

The [claim inventory](CLAIMS.json) separates universal mathematical statements, applications of classical theory and finite computations. [ASSURANCE.md](ASSURANCE.md) describes the final targeted assessment and unresolved boundaries. [REVIEW_RESPONSE.md](REVIEW_RESPONSE.md) records how the review was actioned.

For reproduction use [verification instructions](verification/README.md), [computational assurance](COMPUTATIONAL_ASSURANCE.md), and the [portable weighted-certificate checker](verification/weighted/verify_certificates.py). These distinguish graph-generation trust from exact spectral arithmetic and exact witness validation. [REPLAY_RECEIPT.md](REPLAY_RECEIPT.md) indexes the retained replay evidence; checks run normally and with optimization enabled.

For source context inspect [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), [CITATION_AUDIT.md](CITATION_AUDIT.md), and [RESEARCH_GATES.json](RESEARCH_GATES.json). A bounded negative search is not a novelty proof. Classical generalized diagonal dominance and the ground-state identity are attributed as existing methods.

[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) separates scholarly creator, publisher and agent roles. [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md), [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE) define component reuse terms. [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) records the intended citation; [MANIFEST.sha256](MANIFEST.sha256) binds shipped bytes.
